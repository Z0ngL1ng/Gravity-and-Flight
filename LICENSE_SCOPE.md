# License scope

This release contains the project website, documentation, presentation assets, and six author-provided paper figures with their original PDFs and WebP display versions. The figures comprise two FBA figures and four current G&F figures. It does not include the full manuscript, training/evaluation implementation, model weights, or research datasets. Availability is recorded in [RELEASE_STATUS.md](RELEASE_STATUS.md).

No repository-wide reuse license is granted by this release. A resource being publicly viewable does not by itself establish permission to redistribute it. Resource-specific terms will accompany future research releases.

## Website presentation assets

The website's [abstract field study](web/assets/field-study.svg), [airfoil sketch](web/assets/aerodynamic-study.svg), and [favicon](web/favicon.svg) are original vector illustrations prepared for this project. The project READMEs use the author's supplied paper figures. The two standalone harbor photographs are existing project illustrations used to explain the Anchor–Bridging principle; their inclusion does not constitute release or relicensing of a research dataset. No artwork from the external design-reference collection is bundled.

## Author-provided paper figures

The six figures are published from the author's supplied attachments. [Figure provenance](docs/FIGURES.md) records all six source mappings, original PDF checksums, display derivatives, and interpretive boundaries. The four current G&F figures cover motivation, method, qualitative cases, and token-level response analysis; the two FBA figures document the preceding work's motivation and method.

This publication does not add a separate reuse license for the figures or their embedded material; the font licenses above do not apply to them. The original figure PDFs are available, while the full G&F manuscript remains unreleased.
